# SelfCoaches 🏋️‍♂️✨

> **Be your own coach.** Your ultimate offline-first fitness tracker. A lightning-fast Progressive Web App (PWA) and Android WebView app designed to log your workouts and visualize your strength progression.

🔗 **Try it live :** [selfcoaches.netlify.app](https://selfcoaches.netlify.app)

---

## ⚡ Why SelfCoaches ?

* **100% Local & Private :** All your data stays right on your device using local storage. No accounts, no cloud tracking, no hidden servers.
* **Seamless Migration :** Changing phones? Just export your data to a clean JSON backup and import it back in one click.
* **Zero Friction :** No bloatware, no annoying ads, and absolutely zero subscription paywalls. Completely free, forever.
* **Offline-First :** Works anywhere—even deep in a basement gym with zero Wi-Fi or mobile network.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

* **Custom Routines & Exercises :** Create your own routines, add custom exercises on the fly, and rename them anytime without losing your history.
* **Precise Workout Logging :** Track your **sets, reps, and weight (kg)** with smart auto-fill based on your previous session.
* **Interactive Rest Timer :** Built-in MM/SS rest timer with clean custom steppers to keep your rest intervals strict.
* **Growth Visualization :** Clean, responsive SVG progression charts mapping your weight increases over time.
* **Full Data Control :** Instant local export and import features for complete backup safety.
* **Dual Themes :** Gorgeous dark mode (Black/Blue) and light mode (White/Orange).

---

## 📱 Platforms

* **Web App / PWA :** Install it directly from your browser to your home screen.
* **Android (WebView) :** Can be packaged into a lightweight, lightning-fast native Android app (`.apk`) using Capacitor.
